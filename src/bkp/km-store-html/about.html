WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Local Grocery Store

KM Store Irulam has proudly served the local community since 1996. What began as a small neighborhood grocery shop has grown into a trusted destination for fresh groceries, vegetables, dairy products, dry fish, Kerala spices and household essentials.

Our focus has always been on offering quality products at affordable prices while providing friendly and reliable customer service.

We thank our customers for their continued trust and support over the years.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted grocery store in the region by delivering quality products and excellent customer service.

Our Mission

To provide fresh groceries, vegetables, dairy products and household essentials at fair prices every day.

Our Values

Trust, honesty, quality, freshness and customer satisfaction remain the foundation of our business.
